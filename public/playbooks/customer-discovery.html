PRACTICAL PLAYBOOK · One week

Customer discovery.

Learn what people already do, what breaks, and whether the pain is worth solving.

01

Pick one job title or user group; write a one-sentence hypothesis about a recurring problem.

MAKE THIS: Audience and problem hypothesis.
02

Recruit ten conversations through classmates, campus events, public Purdue contacts, and relevant alumni. Ask for 15 minutes, not a product verdict.

MAKE THIS: Interview list with source and status.
03

Ask: When did this last happen? What did you do? What did it cost in time or money? Who decides to buy a fix?

MAKE THIS: Notes on concrete past behavior.
04

Compare answers. Keep the problem only if multiple people independently describe the same friction and a plausible budget owner exists.

MAKE THIS: Problem evidence and next test.

What to watch out for

  • Leading questions such as 'Would you use my app?'
  • Counting praise as evidence of demand.
