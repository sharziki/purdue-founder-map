PRACTICAL PLAYBOOK · One week to set up; review monthly

Email marketing.

Keep interested people informed without mixing a newsletter with one-to-one research outreach.

01

Decide who the update is for and what they will get, such as a monthly product note or founder event digest. Put that promise beside an opt-in form.

MAKE THIS: Audience promise and signup form.
02

Record how and when each subscriber opted in. Do not import public alumni contacts or cold prospects into a marketing audience without permission.

MAKE THIS: Permission record for each subscriber.
03

Send a welcome note with the promised topic, frequency, and a reply question. Include a working unsubscribe path in each campaign.

MAKE THIS: Welcome message and first update.
04

Send a useful update on a consistent cadence: one finding, one example, one next step. Segment only when interests actually differ.

MAKE THIS: Small, relevant campaign.
05

Review replies, signups, unsubscribes, and purchases. Stop sending content people did not ask for.

MAKE THIS: Monthly channel review.
First email after opt-in
Subject: You're on the [topic] list

Thanks for joining. Every [frequency], I'll send [specific useful content]. This week: [one concrete finding]. What is the hardest part of [problem] for you right now? Reply with one line. You can unsubscribe any time using the link below.

What to watch out for

  • Treating a public email address as newsletter consent
  • Buying or scraping a marketing list
  • Optimizing only for open rate
