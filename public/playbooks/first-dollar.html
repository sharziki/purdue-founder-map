PRACTICAL PLAYBOOK · Two to four weeks

Your first dollar.

Get a buyer to pay for a specific outcome, even if delivery is manual.

01

Choose one buyer with a problem already observed in interviews. State the before-and-after result in plain language.

MAKE THIS: One-sentence offer.
02

Make a small pilot: fixed scope, delivery date, success measure, and price. For research or hardware, a paid feasibility study can be the first offer.

MAKE THIS: One-page pilot proposal.
03

Show the offer to five qualified buyers. Ask directly: 'Would you pay [price] for [result] by [date]?' Record objections without arguing.

MAKE THIS: Buyer objections and willingness-to-pay evidence.
04

For a yes, use an invoice or a payment link, state the terms clearly, and collect payment or a deposit before expanding scope.

MAKE THIS: Paid order or signed paid pilot.
05

Deliver manually if needed, measure the result, and ask what the buyer would purchase again. Use that evidence before seeking a larger grant or investor check.

MAKE THIS: Outcome report and next offer.
AN OFFER TO ADAPT

I will reduce [specific task] from [current effort] to [target] for [buyer] in 14 days. Pilot price: [amount]. Includes [three deliverables]. Success means [observable result].

What to watch out for

  • Calling a free trial revenue
  • Building the entire platform before asking a buyer to pay
  • Changing price or scope after agreement without consent
