THE STARTUP DICTIONARY

Plain English,
please.

The words you’ll hear, without the assumption you already know them.

ARR+

Annual recurring revenue: the annualized value of active recurring subscriptions. One-time sales are excluded.

$1,000 in stable monthly subscriptions corresponds to $12,000 ARR.

Essential SaaS metricsStripe
Accelerator+

A time-bound program that helps a startup make progress through mentorship, peers, and sometimes capital.

A cohort can be useful after you know the customer problem you are testing.

Angel investor+

An individual investing personal money in a company, often at an early stage.

An angel check may fund a specific next milestone; terms still matter.

Burn rate+

How much cash a company spends net of cash it brings in over a period, commonly per month.

If cash falls by $5,000 in a month, net burn was $5,000.

YC's Essential Startup AdviceY Combinator
CAC+

Customer acquisition cost: sales and marketing cost divided by the number of new customers acquired in the same period.

$500 spent to acquire five paying customers gives $100 CAC.

Essential SaaS metricsStripe
Churn+

Customers or recurring revenue lost during a period. State which one you mean.

Two of 20 subscribers leaving in a month is 10% customer churn.

Essential SaaS metricsStripe
Cold outreach+

A direct message to someone who has not previously agreed to speak with you, sent because their work is relevant to a specific question or offer.

Ask an alumnus about a problem they publicly work on; do not add them to a newsletter.

Concierge MVP+

A manually delivered version of a service used to test the promised result before automation.

Create the weekly report by hand for one paying buyer before building a dashboard.

Do things that don't scaleY Combinator
Conversion rate+

The share of people at one step who complete the next action you measure.

Five paid pilots from 50 qualified conversations is a 10% conversation-to-pilot rate.

Essential SaaS metricsStripe
Dilution+

A founder's ownership percentage falls when new shares are issued to investors or employees.

Raising equity can lower each existing holder's percentage even as company value changes.

The SAFEY Combinator
Discovery interview+

A conversation about a person's past behavior, current workflow, and costly problem before pitching a solution.

Ask 'What happened the last time?' instead of 'Would you buy this?'.

Startup School talksY Combinator
Gross margin+

Revenue remaining after direct costs of delivering the product or service, expressed as a percentage of revenue.

$100 revenue minus $30 direct cost gives 70% gross margin.

Essential SaaS metricsStripe
ICP+

Ideal customer profile: a narrow description of the organization or person most likely to benefit from and pay for a product.

Independent machine shops with 5–20 employees and frequent quote delays is more useful than 'manufacturers'.

Startup School talksY Combinator
LTV+

Estimated total value a customer generates over the relationship; early estimates are uncertain.

A $50 monthly customer retained for 12 months yields $600 in revenue before costs.

Essential SaaS metricsStripe
MRR+

Monthly recurring revenue: predictable monthly revenue from active subscriptions, excluding one-time fees.

Ten $20 monthly subscriptions yield $200 MRR.

MVP+

Minimum viable product: the smallest version that lets a real user experience the core value and gives the team evidence.

A working form plus manual fulfillment may test demand before a full app.

Paid pilot+

A limited, paid engagement to test a promised outcome with a real buyer.

A two-week trial with a defined deliverable and price is a paid pilot.

Product-market fit+

Strong evidence that a specific market repeatedly wants and uses a product; there is no single universal numeric threshold.

Customers return, refer peers, and pay again without constant persuasion.

YC's Essential Startup AdviceY Combinator
Runway+

An estimate of how long existing cash lasts at the current net burn rate.

$30,000 cash divided by $5,000 monthly net burn gives about six months of runway.

YC's Essential Startup AdviceY Combinator
SAFE+

Simple Agreement for Future Equity: an investor funds a company now for the right to shares later under agreed terms; it is not a loan.

A SAFE may convert when the company raises a priced equity round.

The SAFEY Combinator
SBIR/STTR+

Federal small-business research programs supporting eligible R&D with commercial potential; STTR involves a research institution partner.

A deep-tech team might assess NSF SBIR/STTR before selling equity.

Traction+

Observed progress showing people want the solution, such as active use, repeat purchases, or paid pilots.

Three buyers paying for the same outcome is stronger evidence than three verbal compliments.

Valuation cap+

The maximum valuation used when a capped SAFE converts to shares in a later priced round.

The cap changes how much ownership an early investor receives at conversion.
